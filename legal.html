What we provide

Terrabox provides the letting and operation of the company's own and leased residential property by TERRABOX NETWORKS LTD, a private limited company incorporated in England and Wales on 28 June 2013 under company number 08589233, whose registered office is at 412 Katherine Road, London, England, E7 8NP. THE COMPANY LETS AS PRINCIPAL AND IS NOT A LETTING AGENT. It does not act as agent for any other landlord, does not market or let property belonging to third parties, and does not hold client money. IT THEREFORE MAKES NO CLAIM TO MEMBERSHIP OF A PROPERTY REDRESS SCHEME OR TO CLIENT MONEY PROTECTION, both of which apply to letting agents rather than to a landlord letting its own property, and prospective occupiers should understand that distinction rather than assume agent protections apply. RIGHT TO RENT: in England a landlord must not authorise an adult to occupy premises as their only or main home under a residential tenancy agreement unless that adult has a right to rent, AND THE CHECK MUST BE CARRIED OUT ON EVERY ADULT AGED 18 OR OVER WHO WILL OCCUPY THE PROPERTY AS THEIR MAIN HOME, WHETHER OR NOT THEY ARE NAMED AS A TENANT, AND BEFORE THE OCCUPATION BEGINS. The company carries out the prescribed check by examining original documents in the presence of the holder, by a Home Office online check where the individual provides a share code, or by identity document validation technology through a certified provider, and carries out follow-up checks where permission to be in the United Kingdom is time limited. COPIES ARE DATED AND RETAINED FOR THE DURATION OF THE OCCUPATION AND FOR ONE YEAR AFTERWARDS, which is what establishes the statutory excuse. A LANDLORD WHO FAILS TO CARRY OUT THE CHECK IS LIABLE TO A CIVIL PENALTY, the maximum for which was increased with effect from 13 February 2024, and a landlord who knows or has reasonable cause to believe that an occupier is disqualified commits a criminal offence punishable by imprisonment. EQUALITY: THE CHECK IS CARRIED OUT ON EVERY PROSPECTIVE ADULT OCCUPIER IN THE SAME WAY AND AT THE SAME STAGE. Carrying out checks selectively, or applying a more demanding standard to a person by reference to their nationality, ethnicity, accent, name or appearance, IS UNLAWFUL DISCRIMINATION, and the company does not require a prospective occupier to produce a particular document where the law permits another, and does not refuse an application on the basis that a person holds time-limited rather than indefinite permission. DEPOSITS AND PERMITTED PAYMENTS: a tenancy deposit taken on an assured shorthold tenancy is protected in a government-approved scheme within the statutory period and the prescribed information is given within that same period; a holding deposit does not exceed one week's rent and may be retained only on the grounds permitted by the Tenant Fees Act 2019; a tenancy deposit does not exceed five weeks' rent where the annual rent is less than £50,000; AND NO PROHIBITED PAYMENT IS REQUESTED OR ACCEPTED, including fees for viewings, for referencing, for the preparation of an inventory or for the drafting of an agreement. SAFETY AND ENERGY: a gas safety record is provided before occupation and annually where there is a relevant gas appliance; the electrical installation is inspected and tested at intervals of no more than five years and the report is provided; smoke and carbon monoxide alarms are provided and tested as required; AND THE COMPANY DOES NOT LET OR CONTINUE TO LET A DOMESTIC PROPERTY WITH AN ENERGY PERFORMANCE CERTIFICATE RATING BELOW BAND E unless a valid exemption is registered on the PRS Exemptions Register, that restriction applying to continued letting and not only to the grant of a new tenancy. THE COMPANY IS NOT A SOURCE OF LEGAL ADVICE and nothing on this site is advice on an occupier's rights or on any landlord's obligations; an occupier in dispute should take independent advice, and free advice is available from local authority housing teams and from advice charities. NO PROPERTY, RENT LEVEL OR AVAILABILITY IS GUARANTEED, availability changes without notice, and rents stated anywhere on this site are indicative and are superseded by the figure in a signed tenancy agreement. Any licence required for a property under a mandatory, additional or selective licensing scheme operated by the relevant local authority is held as stated at [LICENCE TYPE, NUMBER AND ISSUING AUTHORITY, WHERE REQUIRED].
